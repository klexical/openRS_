# Changelog

All notable changes to Sapphire, the openRS_ web dashboard (`web/`), are documented here.
The Android app has its own changelog: [android/CHANGELOG.md](../android/CHANGELOG.md).

Sections, in this order: Added, Fixed, Changed, Removed. Every entry ends with its issue link.
A fix shipped after a slice goes into that slice's Fixed list until the next slice's section opens.

---

## A drive's page (slice 3)

### Added
- **A drive's page keeps one time range, in its address** — a drive's address names its view and its time range, so a reload and the back button keep the place, and "Whole drive" resets the range in one step. Dragging the zoom bar under any of Trip's time charts, or moving it with the arrow keys, sets the range for all of them and for the Dashboard's Trip Summary and Session Peaks; fuel, aggression, thermal progression and the tyre-pressure card are for the whole drive, so while a range is set they are left out and one line says so. The route maps, mode timelines and G-force scatter still show the whole drive, and an address with an unknown view or an unreadable range opens the drive's first view on the whole drive (`lib/selection.ts`, `lib/address.ts`, `DriveFrame.tsx`). ([#416](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/416))
- **Trip shows the drive's thermal record** — a drive imported from an Android export shows, under Thermal Record, the limits each part of the drive was judged against (per profile, with its warn, critical and flag threshold), each sensor's peak with its level and its start and end, and every TEMP flag with the reading behind it, in the chosen units; a reading beside a limit keeps the decimals that put it on the correct side, and a whole limit has no decimal, as in the app. A start or end above the peak is "—"; a drive recorded before limits were saved shows its drive-end readings against the old fixed limits, not as peaks; an export with no record, or one that cannot be read, says "No thermal record". A drive imported before this update shows no record section; deleting it and importing its export again adds one (`lib/thermalRecord.ts`, `ThermalRecordCard.tsx`). ([#315](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/315))

### Fixed
- **A drive mode the app did not know yet is no longer drawn as Normal** — the Android app records "--" while it cannot yet tell Sport from Track (before the first 0x420 frame). The route map's MODE colours and the G-force scatter's mode colours drew those points in Normal's colour; they are now dim, and both legends name them "-- not known yet". The mode timeline already showed "--" as its own segment (`lib/mapColors.ts`, `lib/gforce.ts`). ([#516](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/516))
- **Thermal Progression in the chosen units** — the chart on Trip and the Dashboard now draws its scale, peaks and start-to-peak changes in °F or °C as Settings says, with the unit named, so its oil peak reads the same as the Thermal Record card below it; a change converts as a difference (× 1.8, no +32), as in the app (`ThermalProgressionChart.tsx`). ([#498](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/498))
- **The route map no longer calls every gap in the points a pause** — where more than 5 seconds pass between two points the marker now reads "No points recorded — N s" instead of "Paused — Ns": the points do not say whether the driver paused, the adapter connection was lost or no position fix arrived, so the map names no cause (`GpsMap.tsx`). ([#392](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/392))
- **No fuel figure where the app gives none** — a drive whose export states its fuel used as 0 (the level fell by fewer than four gauge steps, so the app shows "—") now shows no Fuel Used or Economy card, and Compare treats it as a missing reading; before, Sapphire worked out its own figure from the CSV's first and last level. A drive imported without its profile keeps the CSV figure. The fix applies to drives imported from now on; a drive already stored keeps its figure until it is deleted and imported again (`lib/import.ts`). ([#443](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/443))
- **A pause or a missing position update is no longer called a recording gap** — in Diagnostics' Session Events, a REC_GAP event's hover text names the reason its message gives: "Recording gap" only for the adapter-disconnected reason, "Recording paused" for a pause, "No position update" for a missing position update (also the notice written while one is still going on), and "Recording interrupted" for a reason Sapphire cannot read; all keep the warning colour. The Observer's notes column that lists these events is now headed "Recording interruptions" (`lib/eventTypes.ts`, `ObserverNotes.tsx`). ([#393](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/393))

### Changed
- **Diagnostics' frame-rate chart has no zoom bar of its own** — the page's time range belongs to the drive, and the frame-rate chart runs on the session's own time, so it shows the whole session until slice 4 rebuilds Diagnostics (`TimeSeriesChart.tsx`). ([#416](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/416))
- **What's new shows the update it speaks of** — the dialog reads the changelog section whose heading names its own release, not simply the newest section, so it keeps showing slice 2's changes while slice 3's section grows (`lib/changelogSection.ts`). ([#416](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/416))
- **Compare compares the drives that did open** — when a compared drive cannot be read, Compare still names it ("Couldn't open *name*.") and now compares the others; if the first ticked drive is the one that failed, the first that did open becomes the base (in the picker too) and one line says so, and with fewer than two open it says "Compare needs at least two drives that open." (`lib/driveLibrary.ts`, `ComparePanel.tsx`). ([#416](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/416))
- **A Compare card stays when one drive lacks a reading** — each drive shows its own figure, "—" for the drive without it, and no difference or percentage for that pair. A reading never received (a −99 temperature, no boost reading, a fuel figure that could not be computed, no aggression score, the distance of a drive recorded without positions) is never shown as a figure (`lib/compare.ts`, `DeltaCard.tsx`). ([#416](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/416))

## Storage and the list of drives (slice 2)

### Added
- **Home is the list of drives, with addresses** — Sapphire opens on Drives: every stored drive and session-only entry, newest first by when it started, grouped by month, each row with its date, name, duration, distance, top speed and peak boost named by its measurement, in the chosen units. A row opens the drive at its own address with today's Dashboard, Trip and Diagnostics views; the two places, Drives and Settings, are a bar at the bottom on a phone and a rail on a desktop, so reload and back / forward work. The drive views load only when a drive is opened (`lib/address.ts`, `components/drives/`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Import as an action** — a ZIP dropped anywhere on the page imports, and the list's Import button takes several files at once, with real progress (files done of files chosen, and the current file's stage: reading, parsing, storing). One file failing loses none of the others and is named with its file and reason; inside one ZIP all of it is stored or none of it. One new drive lands on that drive's page (whatever the number of files); several new drives land on the list with the new rows marked "New" (`lib/importFiles.ts`, `components/importing/`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **A later import fills in what a stored drive lacks** — the same drive imported again says "already imported" with a link to it, and fills in every part the stored drive is missing: its fault-code scan (also when the stored one could not answer), its session log, and its profile (recorded summary figures, laps, performance runs); a part the stored drive has is never replaced, and its name, note and tags stay (`lib/storage.ts`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Look after a drive from its row** — each row of the list has one menu (its visible menu button, or a right-click with a mouse): rename, note, tags, export (today's trip CSV and full JSON) and delete, all by keyboard alone. Ticked rows offer "Delete selected" (two or more; all of them or none, and a failure says nothing was deleted) and Compare (two to four, against the first ticked). Every delete asks first. The owner's note is kept with the drive (`components/drives/RowActions.tsx`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Find a drive: search, quick filters and markers** — the list of drives gains a search box over name, tags and date, and two quick filters, "With warnings" (labelled "fault codes and gaps" while those are the causes checked) and "With laps", kept when a drive is opened and left. Rows carry the markers "Warnings", "Laps" with the best lap, and "Session only", each a word with a shape; a drive's own page says which warning cause it could not check and what the import lacks. A drive stored before this update shows its best lap once it has been opened (`components/drives/listing.ts`, `lib/warningWords.ts`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Back up all drives, and restore** — Settings writes one backup file holding every drive with its points and diagnostic log, and reads one back; after a failed storage update Sapphire offers a backup of the old drives first. Restore only adds: a drive already here, by id or as the same drive, is skipped and counted, and a file from a newer Sapphire or a damaged one is refused with nothing restored. The file is built in a background worker so the page stays usable (`lib/backup.ts`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Keep my data** — after the first import or restore that stores a drive, Sapphire asks the browser to keep its data, once; Settings says in words whether the browser agreed, and about how much space is used (`lib/keepData.ts`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Match device, Night, Day and Ultra** — Settings' "Palette": Match device, the first visit's choice, follows the device's light or dark setting; Night, Day and Ultra are fixed choices; text on the new screens reads at WCAG AA in all three; on Day, the older Trip, Dashboard and Diagnostics views' fixed yellow, green and orange figures stay below AA until slice 3 rebuilds those views (`styles/lookA.ts`, `lib/devicePalette.ts`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **What's new** — shown once after this update to anyone who had Sapphire before, never on a fresh install, and always reachable from Settings, under About (`components/whatsnew/`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Map background unavailable** — when the map server cannot be reached, a drive's route is drawn on a plain background with the line "Map background unavailable", and every chart still works (`GpsMap.tsx`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **The openRS_ icon in the browser tab** — the favicon is the openRS_ app's launcher icon, in place of the build tool's stock one (`public/favicon.png`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **The fonts ship inside Sapphire** — Barlow, JetBrains Mono and Orbitron (for headings, served as "Sapphire Display") come from Sapphire itself, with their licences, so no font server is asked and the page looks the same with no network; the map background is the one request to another server (`public/fonts/`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))

### Fixed
- **Leaving a route map at once no longer throws a page error** — the map's zoom-to-fit no longer animates, so a drive closed within moments of opening leaves no Leaflet "_leaflet_pos" error behind; the map opens at the fitted view at once (`GpsMap.tsx`). ([#401](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/401))
- **Compare shows a filled-in drive's new figures at once** — when an import fills in a drive that is being compared, Compare reads its drives again, so its differences no longer keep the old values until Compare is reopened (`lib/driveLibrary.ts`). ([#406](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/406))
- **Compare says which drive it could not open** — when a compared drive cannot be read from this browser's storage, Compare shows "Couldn't open <name>." for each such drive instead of saying nothing (`components/panels/ComparePanel.tsx`). ([#407](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/407))
- **Compare leaves out a peak temperature that was never read** — a drive without coolant or oil readings is stored with −99 as its peak (the import's "no reading" marker); Compare showed it as "-99.0 °C" and computed a difference from it, and now shows no Peak Coolant or Peak Oil figure when either drive lacks that reading, as for any missing value (`lib/compare.ts`). ([#414](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/414))

### Changed
- **Drives stored one record each, with a safe upgrade** — Sapphire's database moves to version 2: a small record per drive (the list reads only these), its points and its diagnostic log stored apart and read only when that drive is opened. Drives stored before are converted in place with every field and id kept; if one cannot be converted nothing changes and Sapphire says so, and a second open tab no longer stalls the update. A drive already stored is recognised by its content and not stored twice ("already imported"), and each drive records whether it has warnings (fault codes active or pending, recording gaps) and which causes its import let Sapphire check (`lib/storage.ts`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Settings, rebuilt** — panels with a section index and your drives first, as a summary; "Paint colour" replaces "Accent Color"; Clear all asks in two steps, a backup offered first, and says "Removes every drive and session from this browser. Your settings stay." (`components/settings/`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Compare counts only ticked drives** — the list offers Compare once at least two ticked rows are drives; a ticked session-only entry is left out of it (and stays ticked, for Delete selected), and the list says so (`DrivesPage.tsx`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Compare shows no figures beside a drive it could not open** — when a compared drive cannot be read, Compare now clears the figures it showed before, so only the "Couldn't open <name>." line shows; earlier figures could stay beside it when the same drives were picked again (`lib/driveLibrary.ts`). ([#407](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/407))
- **The paint colours buttons, tabs and highlights only** — figures, sparklines, the boost gauge, Trip's RPM line and histogram, and Diagnostics' ID cells (CAN IDs, DIDs, modules) keep their fixed colours whatever the paint; the old Trip, Dashboard and Diagnostics views keep their layout and take the palette's surfaces and text. ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))

### Removed
- **The Import screen** — Import is an action now (the list's Import button, or a drop anywhere); the screen and the unused batch-ZIP check (`isBatchZip`) are removed (`ImportPanel.tsx`, `lib/import.ts`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **The Sessions screen** — its rename, tags, delete, "Delete selected" and Compare live on in the list of drives (each row's menu, ticked rows); the screen and its search bar are removed (`SessionsPanel.tsx`, `SearchBar.tsx`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **The remembered view and the unused router** — the address is the memory now: the old remembered-view key is ignored, and the `react-router-dom` dependency, which nothing used, is removed (`package.json`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))
- **Unused code** — the data-cell component and the `@` import alias, which nothing used (`DataCell.tsx`, `vite.config.ts`, `vitest.config.ts`). ([#384](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/384))

## Repairs (slice 1)

### Fixed
- **Charts, the peak speed gauge and the notes table follow the units setting** — Trip's Speed, Wheel Speeds, Temperatures, Tire Pressure and Tire Temperature charts, the Dashboard's Peak Speed gauge, the observer's-notes table and the map's note popup now show their values in the selected units instead of km/h, °C and psi under a converted heading; a missing reading stays a gap (`lib/units.ts` `unitChartRows`). ([#381](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/381))
- **Diagnostics events coloured by type** — every event type the app writes now has a group colour, named on hover, matched whatever its letter case; a recording gap shows the warning colour; an unknown type keeps the neutral style (`lib/eventTypes.ts`). ([#382](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/382))
