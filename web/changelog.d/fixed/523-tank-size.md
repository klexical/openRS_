- **Fuel used from a drive CSV alone uses the app's tank size** — a drive imported without its profile (a diagnostic export) now works out its fuel used and economy from a 49.8 L tank, as the app does, instead of 52.3 L, so its figure matches the app's; rows written before the fuel reading arrived stay ignored. The fix applies to drives imported from now on; a drive already stored keeps its figure until it is deleted and imported again (`lib/import.ts`).
