- **A crash mid-drive no longer loses the drive's last points or its fuel and thermal summary** — the recorder wrote points in batches of 30, so a process death lost up to 29 of them, and the drive closed at the next launch kept only what its points held. Every point is now written as it is recorded, and every 10 s the recording drive's row holds the summary STOP would write, so a drive closed after a crash keeps its fuel used, thermal record, fuel trims, pulls, boost peak and weather. Laps of such a drive are still lost, and the drive that auto-record starts after the restart is still a separate drive. (addresses [#512](https://github.com/klexical/openRS_/issues/512))
